How it works

The questions never stop. Neither does she.

A person with dementia can ask the same question forty times in an afternoon. Margaret answers each one warmly and completely, then quietly records what happened so no one has to remember on her behalf.

For the family

You stop guessing how her day went.

Instead of a phone call that tells you nothing, you get what actually happened — what she asked, when she got frightened, what settled her, and whether the tablets went down.

For clinicians

One page a night, written from evidence.

Repetition rate, agitation by hour, medication adherence, and what changed since last week — compiled at eleven, printable, and defensible in a ten-minute appointment. Observations and suggestions for a doctor to review, never a diagnosis.

Privacy

Consent is the first screen, not the small print.

A cloned voice is only ever recorded by the person it belongs to, is used only for comfort messages, and can be deleted by them at any moment. Recordings stay in the family's own store. Nothing is sold, and nothing trains anything.

How it's built

One memory, and the seams to hang services on.

The demo runs entirely in the browser so it never depends on a conference network. Every hosted service in the plan has a named seam in engine.js — search for SEAM: — and the shape of the data either side of it is already right.

Running now, in this browser

The whole loop, offline

Hashed lexical embeddings and cosine retrieval over one memory collection; intent and repeat detection at a 0.85 similarity threshold; a dementia-care rules engine for what she is told; frame-difference and voice-energy analysis of the live camera for mood; the browser's own speech recognition and synthesis; and a BroadcastChannel carrying every write to the dashboard.

What each seam is for

Where the services go

MongoDB Atlas — the store and its change streams. Voyage AI — text and multimodal embeddings. Atlas Vector Search — retrieval and reranking. Fireworks — the fast classifier and the vision model reading the room. Claude via OpenRouter — everything she is actually told, and the nightly report. ElevenLabs — Scribe for her speech, TTS for Margaret's voice, and the consented clone of her daughter's.

Put an OpenRouter key in the browser console — Margaret.Settings.set('openrouterKey', 'sk-or-…') — and the decide step calls Claude with the real system prompt instead of the local rules. If the call fails, it falls back silently, because a demo that depends on a network is not a demo.
