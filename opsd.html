A model teaching its weaker self.

01 The one-paragraph truth

OPSD works — but narrowly, and fragilely.

A single model teaching its weaker self from privileged context gives real early-training, sample-efficient gains. But a large June–July 2026 wave shows the vanilla objective is theoretically ill-posed — privileged-information leakage — and empirically collapses on strong "thinking" models after a few dozen steps.

The single most reliable finding across independent groups: naive full-distribution imitation of a privileged teacher hurts long-horizon reasoning. The fixes that hold up are the ones that stop imitating the teacher directly — use the teacher/student gap only to modulate magnitude while a verifiable reward sets direction.

FINDING 01

Forward KL, not reverse

Contrary to common assumption, the origin paper's default is forward KL, KL(teacher‖student). Its ablation shows reverse KL degrades and JSD gives only limited gains. Full-vocabulary logit matching beats sampled-token — but needs per-token KL clipping so style tokens ("wait", "therefore") don't dominate math tokens.

FINDING 02

The variant space is organized by privilege

Every variant is defined by what serves as privileged information — answer/trace, environment feedback, context/hindsight, or nothing at all. Explore all 30+ below.

FINDING 03

Failure modes independently converged

Leakage, fork suppression, epistemic-verbalization suppression, style drift, OOD collapse — six+ groups arrive at the same story. RLSD's leakage diagnosis is the most independently corroborated result in the field.

FINDING 04

Replication is real but partial

The collapse story is corroborated across groups. But most fixes are reported only by their originating authors, and headline gains are backbone-specific — almost everything is Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B or OLMo-3.

04 What actually holds up

The fixes — and how much to trust them.

A multi-group badge means the approach (or its target failure) has independent corroboration. originator-only means treat the headline number as provisional.

◆ Direction / magnitude decoupling

RLSDmulti-group2604.03128

RLVR reward sets update direction; teacher/student evidence ratio only modulates per-token magnitude. Sampled-token + stop-grad + clipping bounds leakage. Its leakage diagnosis is reused by DemoPSD, CEPO, RLCSD — the field's most validated result.

RLCSDoriginator-only2606.11709

Contrasts the teacher–student gap under a correct vs. wrong hint to cancel the shared style component. Claims stability where OPSD/SDPO "explode or collapse."

CEPO / CASToriginator-only2605.19436 · 2606.00172

Contrastive "does the correct answer favor this token while the wrong one disfavors it" signal. Both show OPSD/SDPO below baseline on 4/5 benchmarks, then fix it.

◆ Sample & token routing

SRPOtarget reproduced2604.02288

Routes correct rollouts to GRPO, failed ones to distillation. Qwen3-8B → 77.4% (+3.4 over GRPO), −17.2% per-step compute. TRACE independently reproduced the collapse SRPO targets.

DemoPSDoriginator-only2607.02502

Disagreement-modulated reverse-KL barycenter: low-disagreement tokens keep dense supervision, high-disagreement tokens get attenuated. Built explicitly as a fix for RLSD's leakage.

SCOPE / GATESoriginator-only2604.10688 · 2602.20574

Perplexity-weighted routing and consensus gating — attenuate the signal wherever teacher and student disagree most.

◆ Restriction / reflection / steering

ROSDoriginator-only2605.28014

Reflection-guided, error-localized, partial-response loss. Dramatic OOD recovery: 41.31% vs. SDPO's 2.88% after ToolUse training on Qwen3-4B.

OGLS-SD / Purifiedoriginator-only2605.12400 · 2607.02234

Outcome-guided logit steering; loss applied only to incorrect rollouts. Purified OPSD subtracts a reference-only teacher and distills a PMI target.

AD-OPSDoriginator-only2607.10805

"Thinking-collapse" diagnosis: an asymmetrical pointwise divergence gate anchors high-risk fork tokens to the frozen base prior. Up to +4.1% over standard OPSD.

◆ Objective corrections & ordering

OPD+originator-only2606.01039

Proves the common stop-gradient reward yields biased f-divergence gradients; gives a corrected gradient-faithful weight.

vOPDoriginator-only2605.07865

Closed-form control-variate baseline: +3% avg over single-sample OPD, matches full-vocab at −57.7% wall-clock.

OPSD-as-compactionoriginator-only2605.06188

The ordering fix: SFT → RLVR → OPSD on correct-only rollouts preserves accuracy and shortens responses. Incorrect-only damages it.

05 Practitioner's decision path

Should you actually use OPSD?

Three questions, distilled from the field's Stage-1→4 recommendations. Answer for your setup.

Do you have a stronger external teacher with logit access?
Is your target a strong long-CoT "thinking" model, or an instruction-tuned / smaller model?
What's your actual goal?
Verdict · use plain OPD, not OPSD

Reach for white-box OPD first.

With a stronger teacher and logit access, ordinary GKD/TRL on-policy distillation is more reliable than self-teacher OPSD. The Thinking-Machines Qwen3 result — 74.4% AIME'24 in 1,800 GPU-hrs vs. RL's 67.6% in 17,920 — is the concrete win here. Save genuine OPSD for when you have no larger teacher but do have privileged info.

Verdict · do NOT run vanilla OPSD

Vanilla OPSD will likely regress at long budgets.

Kaur et al. show up to −17% relative on thinking models at long rollout budgets; Kim et al. show up to −40% OOD. If you still want the signal, use it only as a magnitude modulator with direction anchored to a verifier (RLSD-style), and watch the collapse gauges below like a hawk.

Verdict · a safe place to try it

Small / instruction-tuned models are where OPSD shines.

The largest clean gains are here — Qwen3-1.7B: 43.4 vs. GRPO's 37.7. Start from GRPO, add self-distillation as a magnitude modulator with per-token KL clipping from step one. Prefer sampled-token evaluation when stability is the concern; A/B it against full-vocab for accuracy.

Verdict · the strongest, safest use

Post-RL correct-only compaction. Do this.

The clearest consensus win: run SFT → RLVR → OPSD on correct-only rollouts as a compaction stage. It preserves accuracy while shortening responses. Never use incorrect-only rollouts here — that damages accuracy.

Collapse signatures — abort if you see these

If you run dense distillation, monitor against your GRPO baseline. These are the documented collapse thresholds; hitting any one means change approach.

Actor entropy
> 4×
Per-token entropy exceeding ~4× the GRPO baseline is a documented collapse signature.
Validation drop
> 5–10pp
A fall of 5–10pp from an early peak within ~150 steps — the classic "early gain, then collapse."
Response length
↯ collapse
Sudden length shrink (or runaway explosion) signals style drift overtaking task tokens.

07 Industrial reality check

"On-policy distillation" in the wild ≠ OPSD.

Technical reports frequently conflate the two. The distinction that matters: is the teacher a privileged self, or an ordinary external model?

◆ Shipped, but external-teacher OPD
  • DeepSeek-V4 — replaces its mixed-RL stage with OPD, but it's multi-teacher consolidation from 10+ domain experts. Not privileged self-teaching.
  • Qwen3 / Qwen3-Omni — "Strong-to-Weak Distillation," external teacher.
  • NVIDIA Nemotron-Cascade 2 — multi-teacher, multi-domain "MOPD."
  • GLM-5, MiMo-V2, KAT-Coder-V2… — production OPD, external/lifecycle teachers.
◆ Genuine privileged self-distillation
  • Still overwhelmingly a research technique, not a shipped production recipe.
  • Real gains exist (origin paper, SDPO) but are narrow and backbone-specific.
  • Tooling does support it: TRL's trl.experimental.sdpo, NeMo-RL, veRL/rllm, official code at siyan-zhao/OPSD, lasgroup/SDPO.
  • Do not cite DeepSeek-V4/Qwen3 as production OPSD.
08 Read before you cite

Caveats that change the conclusion.

!

Backbone dependence is severe. Nearly all evidence is Qwen3-1.7B/4B/8B and OLMo-3; almost nothing on Llama, and results flip sign between instruction-tuned and thinking models. Do not assume transfer.

!

Reverse vs. forward KL confusion. Much secondary coverage assumes reverse KL, but the origin OPSD default is forward KL; SDPO uses JSD; RLSD uses sampled-token magnitude modulation. Be precise about which objective a result used.

!

Secondary-source numbers. The widely-quoted "8–12× token efficiency" and "52.2% vs 51.3%" figures come from aggregators, not primary tables (which show 64.8 vs 64.0 on AIME/HMMT avg@12). Treat aggregator figures cautiously.

!

Recency. June–July 2026 papers (DemoPSD, RLCSD, CRPO, Purified OPSD, AD-OPSD, COPSD, OPD+) are single-version preprints; their claims are unreplicated.
